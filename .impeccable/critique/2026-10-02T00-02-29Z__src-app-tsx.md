## Design Specificity Verdict
Unusually authored: chase light from the app's own parser, ghost-segment readouts, step keys, silkscreen labels. Breaks in two places: content contradicts the instrument (template descriptions, wrong phases, impossible baker's %), and red overspend erodes the one organising idea. Detector: source scan 0 findings; in-page 104 on five pages, 99 false positives (cropped plates, tile captions, shell frame); real minor: 1.25 leading in baking mode, ~105-char lines in Settings, h1→h3 skip on dashboard. Overlays: injected in an automated tab, read via console, tab closed.

## Overall Impression
The world is right and the landing page is the best screen. Everything after it undermines it: placeholder copy printed as fact, red spent on six meanings, overlays from the old world. Biggest opportunity is restraint, not new design.

## What's Working
- Landing page: clean under both detectors, argues with the product's own machinery, deliberately un-red CTA.
- Honest degradation where designed in: ghost segments for placeholder timings, Analytics refuses to rank a tie, Notes' error copy.
- Phone baking mode: large mono instruction, one lit key, 44px+ Back/Next bars at thumb height.
