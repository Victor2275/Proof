## Session label check

Every idea carried a label. The session relabelled three and noted one weak citation:

- **X-11 and X-17: relabelled `RULE-BREAK: R14`.** Both spend `--pencil` on something that isn't a margin note from a past bake: the cupboard's skipped items (X-11) and a backup reminder (X-17). R14 gives pencil one meaning. In proposals they keep the break, or appear as a separate within-rules version in ink.
- **X-22: relabelled `RULE-BREAK: R14`.** The `Stamp` and its paprika ring mean a personal best (DESIGN.md · The book). A stamp for an author's credit gives the same mark a second meaning, which is the clash F-06 is about.
- **X-1: its citation is weak, but the label stands.** No rule fixes the phone hero at full width. What X-1 does touch is K-17 and DESIGN.md's thesis that "a recipe reads like a page". The session keeps the RULE-BREAK label because the Provocateur filed it as one, and the owner can rule that no rule is broken.
