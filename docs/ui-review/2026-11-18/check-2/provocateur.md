## Too timid
P-16 and P-18 are the safest proposals left, and they are still too timid. P-16 now has a unit test for plurals and back labels, which is right, but the same slips will come back in copy it doesn't cover. The braver version is one `copy.test.ts` that renders each screen from `e2e/review/screens.ts` and fails on any count of 1 followed by a plural noun, any back label that is not the destination's title, and any phrase on a banned list ("left off" with a finished bake, "Loading"). `designSystem.test.ts` shows this works.

P-18 moves a row. The braver version makes the backup show itself in the place a device-only baker will look. Put "Last exported: never" on the Bakes page's summary line once there are any bakes ("17 bakes · 6 recipes · not backed up"), in plain ink, not a warning colour. It serves R1: the bake log is the one thing the baker can't recreate.

P-10 scored a unanimous 5, and it is the least bold proposal that did. Numbering tiles is right, but F-27 asks a harder question. The sparkline is flat for the cheesecake, and a trend drawn from five identical stars says nothing. The braver version is to drop the sparkline wherever it is flat and print "3★ every time" on its own, so the strip's header carries a fact instead of a drawing. P-10 keeps the drawing for non-flat cases and that is enough, but it should say so, and the "flat" test should live in code beside the R11 tie rule.

P-04 (empty rows vanish) still blocks a recipe with no title. The braver version saves it as "Untitled recipe" and puts the title error away for good. The editor then has no error state, F-02 can't come back, and the draft system already covers the person who didn't mean to save.
