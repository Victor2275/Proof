# Librarian, round 1

## Headline
There are plenty of ways in, but on a phone most of them sit several screens down, and the one that matters at 200 recipes has no live proof yet. The top-of-page search is good: it says "Search 203 recipes, ingredients, notes", and the "that rye loaf from the summer" case depends on it. Browsing is weak. Most tiles in the grid are blank cream squares, titles are cut off on the phone, and Sort, Filter and Grid/List are the last thing on the page. The Shop to-buy list was captured empty, so I can't confirm that the planning goal works. The cupboard is a bare list of three words, and nothing in the shots connects it to a shopping list. Navigation chrome is clean and consistent. Export and import are findable, but only through the avatar and then a long Settings page. I read all the `facts.json` files and none lists an unmocked endpoint, console error, broken image or overflow. The blank plates are therefore not a load failure.

Scenario answers:
1. **Find it.** Ways in from the cookbook's first screen, with the phone depth:
   - Search field: top, 1 tap (`librarian__regular-cookbook__light__w375__fold.png`).
   - "Recently baked" strip: about one screen down. It has no way to filter by season and runs to only 5 bakes.
   - "Chapters / A–Z" contents toggle: at the fold's lower edge. The chapters (Dessert 44, Side 31 and so on) sit below the fold, and 5 of them hide behind "5 more chapters and loose notes".
   - "Sort" (Recently added, A–Z, Recently baked, Rating), "Filter" and "Grid / List": about 2.5 screens down on the phone.
   - Desktop top bar: "Search or jump to… Ctrl K". The page's own search field repeats it.
   - There is no date or season filter, so "from the summer" only works if the search matches notes or dates.
2. **Scan 200.**
   - Phone grid: 2 columns, about 6 tiles per screen and 4–5 screens for 35 tiles.
   - Desktop grid: 4 columns, about 8 tiles per screen.
   - Only the 3 baked recipes have photos in the grid; every other tile is an empty cream square.
   - Titles are cut off on the phone: "Chicken wings with cumin, lemon &...", "Chocolate Raspberry...", "Khobz el Dar (Algerian Semolin...". Desktop wraps to 2 lines.
   - No timings appear on any tile or row, so the 50-minute placeholder is not shown as measured. That is honest. The only times shown are in the editor preview ("Prep 20 min · Cook 1 h 15" for the cheesecake).
3. **Fix one.** Recipe page → edit is one tap. On a phone the ingredient quantities are then about 2 screens down, past Title, "A line about it", Photographs and a collapsed "More details". On desktop they are half a screen down, with a live preview beside them. Whether saving is safe is unproven; see LB-6 and the live checks.
4. **Plan the week.** See LB-7. I can't judge it.
5. **Get around.**
   - Destinations: 4 (Cookbook, Bakes, Shop, Discover).
   - Phone: a bottom tab bar, with the avatar at the top right of each page.
   - Desktop: a top bar with pill tabs, search, "New" and the avatar.
   - The avatar menu holds Settings, Sign in, "My published recipes" and Help.
   - Export and Import are in Settings → "Your cookbook" (`librarian__regular-settings__light__w1280__text.txt`). That is avatar, then Settings, then a scroll.
   - The 404 reads "p. 404 / That page isn't in this book." with a search and "Back to the cookbook".
6. **Notes.** See LB-9.
