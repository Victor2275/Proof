# Link Follower, round 1

## Headline
As a recipe, the shared page works. The amounts, the method and the timer buttons are all there, and the ingredients carry metric figures. As a way into the app it is good too. "Save to my cookbook" is clear, and "No account needed" is true. Saving worked at once and the recipe was in my Cookbook right after. Signing up lost nothing. The weak spots are cooking tonight and finding more. The page never says how long the whole thing takes, because the overnight chill is missing from the header. It also never offers "more like this". The shared page and the saved copy look almost identical, so it's hard to tell which one you're on.

## What I could not judge
- I did not see any publish, comment or report refusal. The brief says they are stubbed, and none of my steps led there. I did not tap "Sign in to rate it" or "Report this recipe".
- I did not check dark mode live. I read the dark phone shot, which looks fine, with a legible paprika button.
- The desktop shared-link shot (`link-follower__stranger-shared-link__light__w1280__fold.png`) puts ingredients under the photo and the method on the right, with Method visible at once. That is a good layout for a kitchen counter. I did not walk the 1280 width live.
- I did not try the native share sheet, because it doesn't exist in this browser. I did not try QR code, PDF or print.
- I could not tell whether the first Share tap really did nothing or was caught mid-animation.
