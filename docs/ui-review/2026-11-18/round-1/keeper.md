# Keeper, round 1

## Headline
The redesign works where it counts. The bake log is visible on the cookbook home, the recipe page, Bakes and baking mode. The paper, ink and single-paprika language is held consistently at both widths and in both themes. The strongest assets are the recipe page, which carries "Last time" and the pencil note, the Bakes journal with its By recipe view, and baking mode's one-step-one-button layout. The cookbook home's "Pick up where you left off" card, the honest data rules, the empty states, and the shell with its four destinations also work. Fixes aimed at the real friction (the empty Shop, the bare sign-up, the wordy shared-link footer) must not flatten these, and every item below is something a "make it more consistent" or "add more" pass could quietly destroy. Evidence is the fold shots. I did not open the page shots or facts files beyond what the brief states: facts.json is clean on every screen.
