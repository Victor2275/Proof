# First-Timer, round 1

## Headline
Yes, it's worth five minutes. I understood the app from the landing page, got into the cookbook with one tap and no sign-up, wrote a recipe, baked it and logged a bake with a note. The recipe page then showed my note back to me, which is the thing my friend meant. The friction is in the editor, where an empty row quietly blocks saving and "Write it" opens a screen that looks like a mix of three tools. Smaller gaps are the "Done" vs "Log this bake" buttons at the end of the bake, and no units setting. I would come back for the "Next time" note on the recipe, not for the settings.

## What I could not judge
- Pasting a whole ingredient list. My tool types text but can't paste, so the "splits into rows" feature is untested.
- Photograph import, "Tidy up with AI", and the URL import. I didn't try them. The Cookbook "Paste a link or text" door is marked in the shots but not walked.
- The timer counting down, voice, "Wave to advance", haptics and the notification button. They need a real phone.
- The desktop screens beyond the three fold shots I read (landing, empty cookbook, new recipe). They looked tidy and matched the phone, with the top bar in place of the tab bar.
- The two console errors that showed on every page of my walk. I couldn't see their text and they caused no visible fault.
