## Summary of what matters most to me
- P-10, P-07 and P-06 are the proposals that move the bake log: attempt numbers on every bake, the "Next time" line surviving, and a log sheet that leads with the writing.
- P-01 needs the amendment so that the first ingredient is reachable from the first screen, and it must ship with P-07 so the "Last time" card can grow by a line without pushing "Start cooking" down.
- Reject anything that moves "+ Log bake" or "Last time" (P-19, and P-02 as drawn).
