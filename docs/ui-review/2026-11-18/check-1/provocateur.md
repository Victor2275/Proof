## Too timid
P-16 (labels) is the safest proposal here and it undersells the fix. It is a list of six edits to strings, and the next screen will drift again. The braver version is a test, not a patch. Add one unit test, `labels.test.ts`, that renders every screen and fails the build on three things: a count of 1 that ends in a plural noun, a back label that isn't the title of the page it goes to, and a card label that says "left off" for a finished bake. `designSystem.test.ts` already enforces tokens this way. That makes copy a rule, not a one-off fix.

P-15 (sign-up receipt) is next. A toast that says "Check your email" is still a message the user has to act on alone. The braver version is a first-run card on the cookbook, shown once after sign-up: "Publish a recipe" as one whole-card row and "Back up your cookbook" as a second. It is not a welcome screen. It names the two things an account is for.

P-04 (empty rows vanish) is right but stops at the row. Add the same rule to the whole editor: nothing blocks saving except a missing title, and even that is solved by saving as "Untitled recipe". Then the editor has no error state at all, and F-02 can't recur.
