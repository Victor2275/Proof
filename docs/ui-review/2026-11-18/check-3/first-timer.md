## What changed my mind
No verdict changed this round. Round 3 changed only wording on the proposals I voted on. P-05 and P-24 gained placement notes, and P-01 absorbed P-21. None of them gave me a reason to move. P-02, P-19 and P-20, which I rejected, were dropped, and that matches my votes.

## My top 10
1. P-04 — an empty row stopped me saving and the only message was a browser bubble. This is the clearest fix for getting a first recipe in.
2. P-05 — "Write it" should open a blank page. It's the biggest change to my first five minutes in the editor.
3. P-06 — the note comes first in the log sheet and "Done" no longer looks like save. The bake log is why I'd come back.
4. P-17 — it tells me where I am in a bake. The two bars alone read as a step counter.
5. P-16 — small copy slips (plural, "Pick up where you left off", "your phone" on desktop) that make a new app feel unfinished.
6. P-24 — a plain "Ready by…?" line replaces a long caveat I would have skipped.
7. P-03 — the header stops implying an 8-hour cake takes 1 h 35.
8. P-13 — a sort and filter button that appears only when there's something to sort.
9. P-07 — "Next time" and "Changed:" follow me. That's what I liked most about the recipe page.
10. P-01 — a smaller photo and the keys higher up, for when I'm cooking tonight.
