# New user (home cook, weekend baker, blind to the code)

## Headline
I arrived from a friend's link wanting to read a cheesecake recipe. What I got looked like a piece of audio gear from 1982 that happened to contain a cheesecake. The recipe is there and the photo is lovely, but the first thing I see is a huge all-caps title, pale grey monospace prose, glowing seven-segment digits and a row of two dead grey boxes. It doesn't feel like a cookbook. It feels like a dashboard for an oven. The idea underneath (log each bake and get better) is good, but I only found it by reading the landing page, which I only reach by going out of my way. On a phone the screens are dense, the type is wide and tiring, and the one thing I want to do (save the recipe, or just cook it) is at the bottom of a long scroll or hiding under a floating "+".

## First-impressions diary

**Shared recipe link (stranger-05, phone).** A black screen, a back arrow saying "DISCOVER" and three dots. Then SHARED BY @PROOF, which I do not know. I read a description, then see a photo (finally something I want) next to digital clock numbers. I think: is this a timer app? I scroll. I find two dead grey boxes and a sentence cut off by a button. The red button is there but half hidden. It did not feel like a cookbook; it felt like a control panel. Ingredients: "Not in your pantry" on every line. I do not have a pantry, so I feel I have done something wrong. Method: numbered steps with "READ MORE" which makes me wonder what is being hidden. I'd have left.

**Landing (stranger-01).** "PROOF" huge. A paragraph about patterns and machines that I do not follow. A box with a row of coloured keys labelled "LEVAIN — NOW". Is this for bread? Music? I see "OPEN THE COOKBOOK" and "NO ACCOUNT. NOTHING TO SET UP." I like that second part. The three feature cards are readable, "Hands free" and "Timers that follow" sound like real kitchen problems (floury hands), which is nice, and "Every bake, kept" is the first thing that sounds new. But it is in tiny grey print at the bottom.

**Onboarding (stranger-02).** A dark pop-up, "1 OF 3", "BROWSE & DISCOVER". It talks about folders and tags. I did not ask to organise anything. I have nothing in here. I wanted to tap "Skip" but it looks like a label.

**Empty cookbook (stranger-03).** "MY COOKBOOK" then "START FROM ONE OF THESE". Pleasant: real food photos. I tap SAVE on the cheesecake in my head. But SAVE is a heavy black rectangle. Blank page / write a recipe is at the very end. A red "+" floats over the photo and I do not know what it adds.

**Discover (stranger-04).** An offline-error message. I feel like I broke something. The sort tabs are cramped and "A–Z" breaks over two lines. I can't tell what Discover would have shown me.

**Profile (stranger-06).** The nicest one. Big pictures, a name, "The house cookbook." Numbers 12 published and 40 baked. I immediately want to tap a photo. Nothing tells me how to follow or save the whole cookbook. The "+" button is on top of a tile title again.

**A week later: dashboard (regular-01).** "203 RECIPES." Honestly overwhelming: I did not choose 203 recipes. The same cheesecake three times. The bake history I have been writing for a week is invisible. The filter chips run off the edge. The red "+" floats over the second photo.

**Recipe (regular-02).** Huge caps title, glowing digits, two grey boxes, "LOCAL LIVE EDITS ONLY ON THIS DEVICE", PUBLISH. I understand "publish", but "live edits" I do not. The red play button is the right idea, just unlabelled.

**Baking mode (regular-03).** My favourite: big step text, Back/Next. I would use it with messy hands. Slightly cluttered header, and no sign of where my notes go.

**More menu (regular-14).** Clean and simple. Fine.

## Words I didn't understand
- **Bake** (as a noun for a logged attempt): not obvious until I find "Every bake, kept".
- **"A recipe is a pattern. A bake is that pattern running"**: poetry, not explanation.
- **"Where now is" / "COOK — NOW" / "Phase 01 of 07"**: what is a phase? What is "now"?
- **PUBLISH** vs **SAVE** vs **SHARE**: three verbs for similar things.
- **LOCAL LIVE EDITS / ONLY ON THIS DEVICE**: sounds technical.
- **Baker's %**: I know it as a bread thing, but it appears on a cheesecake with no explanation.
- **COPY LIST / SCALE 0.5X–3X**: scale is OK, "copy list" copies what?
- **Personal best**: a baking record? Of what? (rating?)
- **Pantry** and "Not in your pantry": I never made a pantry.
- **Analytics**: for a home cook this sounds like work.
- **General notes**, **Gallery**: gallery of what? (turns out bake photos)
- **Inspire me** (the shuffle icon on phone has no label at all).
- **The Bluetooth icon in baking mode**: why Bluetooth?
- **PROOF**: bakers know "proofing" dough; non-bakers won't; there is no tagline saying so.

## What would have made me stay
1. A one-sentence promise on the landing: "Your recipes, and what happened every time you made them." Plus a real photo of a loaf, not a drum machine.
2. A shared-recipe page for strangers: title, photo, times in one plain line, a visible "Save to my cookbook" button above the fold, and no member nav or tab bar.
3. Plain, readable type. Sentence-case headings in a warm serif, body in a comfortable sans at 16 px+, no all-caps mono for paragraphs. Make it feel like a printed page.
4. Replace the seven-segment digits with normal numbers ("Prep 20 min · Cook 1 h 15 · Serves 12"), and remove ghost "8" segments.
5. Delete the unlabelled grey boxes and coloured key strips from recipe and share pages, or label them clearly. They look like broken images.
6. Put the bake log on the dashboard and on every recipe: "Last baked 3 days ago · 4★ · 'less sugar'" with a labelled "Bake again" button. Show a small photo strip of past attempts on the recipe page.
7. Replace the floating "+" and red play square with labelled buttons ("New recipe", "Start baking") and never let them cover text or photos.
8. Show each recipe once on the dashboard. Remove duplicate rows; show "Personal best" as a badge.
9. Collapse the filters into one "Filter" control on phone, keep everything inside the margins, and fix the squashed Discover tabs.
10. Clean imported recipe data on display: no "((170g))" double brackets, no "--- Cheesecake ---" as a step, no "0.51 fl oz", no "READ MORE" truncation; show all steps and notes.
11. Hide "Not in your pantry" until the user has a pantry, and say what a pantry is the first time.
12. Replace the feature tour with one first action: "Pick a recipe to bake first", and make Skip a proper button.
13. In baking mode, show the step's ingredients and any timer on the step, drop the Bluetooth icon, and finish with a "How did it turn out?" screen (rating, photo, note) so the log happens naturally.
14. Tell me once, plainly, near the top of my cookbook: "Your recipes stay on this phone. Back them up any time." Instead of a grey footnote.
15. Make Discover work when offline with a cached sample and a short, friendly empty state.
