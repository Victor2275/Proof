# Pessimist reviewer

All filenames are in `audit-shots/overhaul/before/`. Shorthand: `dash` = regular-01-dashboard, `recipe` = regular-02-recipe, `bake` = regular-03-baking-mode, `landing` = stranger-01-landing, `shared` = stranger-05-shared-recipe-link. "375" and "1280" are the capture widths.

## Headline
Proof does not look like a cookbook. It looks like audio hardware that happens to hold 203 recipes. All-caps letter-spaced monospace is used for almost everything: nav, labels, buttons, chips, and even recipe titles in the Gallery and grocery picker. Nothing reads comfortably, and no screen has a warm or editorial moment. The bake log is supposed to be the main character, but it has no nav entry. It is split across a "Previous makes" tab, a screen called "Gallery" whose title is "Bake History", and a recipe-page section whose text dump shows only a source URL. The dashboard is a wall of near-identical tiles, with the same cheesecake shown up to three times. Empty grey "step key" boxes, a red FAB that follows you onto every screen, and decorative seven-segment digits add noise without helping anyone find or share a recipe.

## Systemic problems
1. **Uppercase tracked monospace everywhere.** Nav, chips, buttons, labels, titles and prose all use it. It hurts reading speed and kills the cookbook feel (8, 13, 19, 56, 86).
2. **Instrument metaphor leaks into content and copy.** Seven-segment digits, "lamps", "NOW", "phase", "machine", "fuzzy match" and "live edits" describe the design, not the baker (9, 20, 22, 47, 53, 58, 67).
3. **Decorative hardware elements stand in for information.** Step keys, segment bars, the 3-bar meter and panel-header lamps carry no data and read as empty or broken (1, 2, 33, 48, 49, 75).
4. **Every screen is a stack of bordered panels.** Boxes in boxes, hairlines and 3 to 4 line intros push content below the fold (15, 40, 75, 76).
5. **The bake log is not structurally central.** No "Bakes" nav, a tab and a screen with different names, and "Lab notes" holding only a source URL (5, 7, 43, 46, 84, 85).
6. **Navigation and the FAB do not match tasks.** More is a junk drawer, Analytics is hidden, and the FAB follows you everywhere and covers content (3, 50, 51, 80).
7. **Data quality shows through.** Placeholder timings in precision displays, decimal ingredient amounts, "---" steps, HTML entities, import boilerplate and test notes (21, 26, 27, 28, 57, 83).
8. **Colour is overloaded.** Red means "now", "primary action" and "new". Olive keys look like mud (78).
9. **No warmth and no photography in the chrome.** True black and putty beige, a type-only landing, a type-only profile. It does not look like a cookbook (9, 70, 71, 77).
10. **Sharing is an afterthought.** Hidden in a kebab on phone, a shared-link page that is a spec sheet with its CTA covered, and a profile with no identity (4, 24, 71, 72).

## The ten worst things
1. The recipe page is a hardware dashboard: ingredients sit far below the fold, and the bake log it should feature holds only a source URL (#6, #7).
2. The bake log has no top-level home: three names, a hidden tab and no "log a bake" action (#5, #84).
3. Empty grey boxes: the recipe step keys and the Gallery's photo-less bakes look broken (#1, #2).
4. All-caps tracked monospace for everything, including prose and recipe titles (#8, #19).
5. The "+" FAB floats over every screen and hides content, including the share CTA (#3, #4).
6. The landing page sells a metaphor with a ghost-button CTA and a "PHASE 88 OF 07" readout that looks broken (#9, #67, #68).
7. Placeholder timings and machine-scaled ingredient numbers ("0.51 fl oz", "0.13 tsp") presented as precise readings (#21, #26, #83).
8. Dashboard: duplicated tiles, a lone Personal Best, a clipped chip row and a 48-tile "show more" for 203 recipes (#10, #11, #12).
9. "More" is a junk drawer that buries Analytics, Pantry, Grocery and Account (#50, #51).
10. Shared-recipe and profile pages are not made to be shared: spec sheet first, CTA covered, no avatar (#4, #71, #72).
